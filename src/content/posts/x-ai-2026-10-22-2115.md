## AI 热点
1. **Microsoft Research: humans directing AI agents explore 100+ ideas and prove the 31-year-old KLS conjecture**: The Kannan–Lovász–Simonovits conjecture (1995) asks whether the best hyperplane cut of a high-dimensional log-concave distribution is within a dimension-independent constant of the best cut of any shape, which determines how fast random-walk sampling in high dimensions can be. Microsoft Research's Xinzhi Zhang and Zhao Song give a constant bound; per the Microsoft blog, AI agents under researcher direction explored over 100 candidate routes, ran derivations, helped draft and cross-check, while the researchers did the selection, pruning and final argument. Bizeul, Klartag and Lehec have posted an independent proof on arXiv based on the same criterion, and a Lean formalization exists. Another hard-core case of "AI as research assistant, human at the wheel". https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/blog/solving-a-31-year-old-grand-challenge-in-mathematics-the-kls-conjecture/ ｜ https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05474 ｜ https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2113151168176443568
> [zh] **微软研究院：人类指挥 AI agent 探索 100 多条思路，证明 31 年悬而未决的 KLS 猜想**：Kannan–Lovász–Simonovits 猜想（1995 年提出）问的是高维对数凹分布里「最好的平面切割」是否总和「最好的任意形状切割」只差一个与维数无关的常数，它决定了高维随机游走采样能有多快。微软研究院的 Xinzhi Zhang 与 Zhao Song 给出了常数界；按微软博客的说法，AI agent 在研究者指挥下探索了 100 多条候选路线、做推导、帮写稿和内部检查，研究者负责筛选、剪枝和最终论证。Bizeul、Klartag、Lehec 已在 arXiv 发了基于这一判据的独立证明，也有人做了 Lean 形式化。这是「AI 当研究助手、人类掌舵」的又一个硬核案例。

2. **Coding-agent sandboxes come in three layers, and what belongs inside**: Paul Iusztin implemented sandboxing in Decode, a coding agent he wrote from scratch. Layer one is OS-level sandboxing (macOS Seatbelt, Linux Bubblewrap) sharing the host kernel; layer two is a user-space kernel such as gVisor that intercepts syscalls; layer three is microVMs such as Firecracker with their own kernel, the strongest isolation. His advice: keep the harness local and put only Bash, Read, Write and Edit in the same sandbox so they see one filesystem; Decode uses one executor interface for local, Docker and Modal remote sandboxes. https://x.com/pauliusztin_/status/2113246493763088553
> [zh] **编码 agent 的沙箱分三层，以及该把什么放进沙箱**：Paul Iusztin 在自己从零写的编码 agent Decode 里实现了沙箱：第一层是系统级沙箱（macOS 的 Seatbelt、Linux 的 Bubblewrap），共享宿主内核；第二层是 gVisor 这类用户态内核，拦截系统调用；第三层是 Firecracker 等 microVM，有独立内核，隔离最强。他的建议是 harness 留在本地，只把 Bash、Read、Write、Edit 这些工具放进同一个沙箱，保证它们看到同一个文件系统；Decode 用一个执行器接口同时支持本地、Docker 和 Modal 远程沙箱。
