## 有趣用法和干货
1. **复杂 agent 记忆不一定必要：朴素 RAW-RAG 在长对话记忆评测上更强**：只把原始对话切成 256 token 的块（32 重叠）做向量检索，取 top-128 再用 cross-encoder 重排到 top-15 交给模型，在 LoCoMo、LongMemEval、MEME、MemoryAgentBench 的 7 组「基准×模型」里 6 组第一，领先最强的 agentic memory 系统 0.13–5.53 分，BEAM-1M 摄入只要 0.3 小时（Mem0、MemOS 等要 3.2–27 小时）。关键发现：瓶颈在证据够不够，改写成记忆条目会丢掉关系和依赖路径；Mem0 的 UPDATE/DELETE 只占写操作的 4.39%，关掉反而更准。给 agent 加记忆层之前，先在自己数据上跑一遍这个基线。https://x.com/shao__meng/status/2113803928869744647 ｜ https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30755
> [en] **Complex agent memory isn't always needed: plain RAW-RAG wins on long-conversation memory benchmarks**: chunk the raw conversation into 256-token windows (32 overlap), retrieve top-128 by embedding, rerank to top-15 with a cross-encoder and hand that to the reader; it ranks first in 6 of 7 benchmark×model settings across LoCoMo, LongMemEval, MEME and MemoryAgentBench, beating the strongest agentic memory system by 0.13–5.53 points, and ingests BEAM-1M in 0.3 hours versus 3.2–27 hours for Mem0, MemOS and others. Key finding: the bottleneck is whether the evidence is there at all, and rewriting history into memory entries drops relations and dependency paths; Mem0's UPDATE/DELETE make up only 4.39% of writes, and turning them off actually improved accuracy. Run this baseline on your own data before adding a memory layer to your agent.

> [en] **Awesome Short Drama Skills: an AI short-drama skill list backed by a controlled test**: 83 hand-checked short-drama skills and 16 related open-source platforms, organized by stage from novel adaptation, episode outlines and hook design to script review, storyboards and video prompts for Seedance, Kling and MiniMax. The author ran two rounds of tests: 10 script skills plus a no-skill control group, each writing the same brief 3 times, judged blind by AI; only 5 beat the control all three times, and the limits are stated honestly (end-of-episode hooks were often better without any skill). Works with any skill-capable agent, including Claude Code, Codex, Qwen and Doubao.
