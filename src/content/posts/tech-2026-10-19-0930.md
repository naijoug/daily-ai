## 新产品 / 开源
1. **HybridAgent: an agent that thinks in latent space and calls tools in text**: a paper and open-source code from Stanford, Together AI and UIUC. A lightweight HybridLink lets the model switch between "latent reasoning" and "text interaction" within a single generation, keeping continuous working memory across turns, with tool calls still in text and latent thinking still interpretable. For training, HybridSFT stabilizes it first, then HybridRL learns when to think in latent space and when to act in text. The paper reports an average 10.4% improvement across 3 model sizes and 11 math, science, medicine, search and agent benchmarks, with 35.5%–54.7% fewer tokens, 1.8–2.6x faster inference and 2.7–3.7x lower cost. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18861) ｜ [GitHub](https://github.com/togethercomputer/HybridAgent) ｜ [HN discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50145337) (about 200 points. Some press for benchmark details, wanting to know whether it only saves tokens or really improves accuracy; one imagines a more radical approach of passing latent vectors alongside text in text mode too; others object to describing LLMs as "thinking")
> [zh] **HybridAgent：在隐空间里想、用文本调工具的 Agent**：斯坦福、Together AI、UIUC 的论文和开源代码。它用一个轻量的 HybridLink 让模型在同一次生成里在「隐式推理」和「文本交互」之间切换，多轮对话间保留连续的工作记忆，调工具仍走文本，隐式思考也可以被解读。训练上先用 HybridSFT 稳住，再用 HybridRL 学会什么时候在隐空间里想、什么时候用文本动手。论文报告在 3 个模型规模、11 个数学、科学、医学、搜索和 Agent 基准上平均提升 10.4%，token 少 35.5%–54.7%，推理快 1.8–2.6 倍，成本低 2.7–3.7 倍。[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18861) ｜ [GitHub](https://github.com/togethercomputer/HybridAgent) ｜ [HN 讨论](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50145337)（约 200 分。有人追问基准细节，想知道它只是省 token 还是真能提准确率；有人设想更彻底的做法，文本模式下也同时传入隐向量；也有人反对用「思考」形容 LLM）
2. **Free Pascal and Lazarus finally get dynamic packages**: the author says the feature was held up in the FPC ecosystem for over 20 years. A dynamic package is an ordinary shared library with extra metadata (supported on Linux, Windows and macOS), so installing components and IDE extensions no longer requires recompiling all of Lazarus, and your own programs can use it as a plugin system with runtime replacement; the runtime also handles passing threadvars, strings and dynamic arrays between the main program and packages. [Website](https://www.getlazarus.org/dynamic/) ｜ [HN discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50144066) (someone points out this site is a Lazarus fork and the official one is lazarus-ide.org; another complains FPC still requires declaring variables at the start of a block)
> [zh] **Free Pascal 和 Lazarus 终于有了动态包**：作者称这个功能在 FPC 生态里拖了 20 多年。动态包就是带额外元数据的普通共享库（Linux、Windows、macOS 都支持），装控件和 IDE 扩展不用再重新编译整个 Lazarus，自己的程序也能用它做插件系统，运行时可以替换；运行时还处理了线程变量、字符串和动态数组在主程序和包之间传递的问题。[官网](https://www.getlazarus.org/dynamic/) ｜ [HN 讨论](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50144066)（有人指出这个站是 Lazarus 的一个分叉，官方是 lazarus-ide.org；也有人抱怨 FPC 至今仍要在块开头声明变量）

5. **RepRapMicron (μRepRap): a DIY micron-scale 3D printer from common materials**: the goal is a self-replicating RepRap with micron or even sub-micron precision, with the flexure structure drawn from the OpenFlexure microscope stage. The currently recommended PIKA V0.02 achieves about 1 μm repeatability and under 10 μm accuracy in roughly an 8 mm square workspace, has deposited 4 μm UV-resin droplets, and printed a 0.5 mm Benchy at 4 μm layer height. The same kind of direction as the Open MicroManipulator covered in last night's issue. [Project wiki](https://github.com/VikOlliver/RepRapMicron/wiki) ｜ [HN discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50141076) (329 points. Someone compares it to the late 18th century when Ramsden and Maudslay turned precision graduation and threads from craft into industry; another grumbles the project's USB microscope is poor quality; another jokes "in 18 months it'll be making its own RAM")
> [zh] **RepRapMicron（μRepRap）：用常见材料 DIY 微米级 3D 打印机**：目标是做一台能自我复制、精度到微米甚至亚微米的 RepRap，柔性结构来自 OpenFlexure 显微平台的思路。目前推荐的 PIKA V0.02 在大约 8 mm 见方的空间里能做到约 1 μm 的重复定位和 10 μm 以内的精度，沉积过 4 μm 的 UV 树脂液滴，用 4 μm 层高打出了 0.5 mm 的 Benchy。和昨晚那期写过的 Open MicroManipulator 是同一类方向。[项目 Wiki](https://github.com/VikOlliver/RepRapMicron/wiki) ｜ [HN 讨论](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50141076)（329 分。有人类比 18 世纪末 Ramsden、Maudslay 把精密刻度和螺纹从手艺变成工业；也有人吐槽项目用的 USB 显微镜质量很差；还有人调侃「18 个月后就能自己造内存了」）

1. **Anthropic-backed AI safety bill: a model can only be halted when it could cause 50+ casualties or $1B in losses**: per Politico (via Tom's Hardware), the bill requires independent third parties registered with the Commerce Department to regularly assess labs and models, with risk limited to bioweapons, AI-driven cyberattacks and lab loss of control. A risk found by assessors must be reported to Commerce within 72 hours, and Commerce then hands the evidence to a judge at the US District Court for the District of Columbia to decide whether to pause release; it also stipulates that no federal department other than Commerce can order a lab to stop releasing a model. Anthropic says this is the "Advanced AI Framework" it has lobbied for since June.
